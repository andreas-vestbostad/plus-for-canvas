# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of **Plus for Canvas** (called Plus inside Canvas).

* **Command palette** (<kbd>⌘K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd>): courses, course pages, modules, assignments, files, discussions, announcements and quizzes. Courses answer to their code or number, with your own nicknames, and frequent destinations rank higher.
* **Deadlines** in Canvas' menu, with a count and a countdown: what is left (late work first), recently handed in and recently graded. Archive what you do not need. `due` / `frist` shows the same list in the palette.
* **Home+**, an overview page inside Canvas: late work, the next two weeks, new grades, today's lectures and your courses with score, next deadline and **Continue**. Cards can be hidden and put in your own order.
* **Menu layout:** hide and reorder items in Canvas' menu, and an optional wide sidebar with your courses.
* **Dark mode** (built on Dark Reader), **colour themes** and a cleaner calendar month view.
* Feature switches in a **Plus** tray in Canvas' menu and on the settings page.
* Works on `*.instructure.com` and `mitt.uib.no`; other Canvas domains can be added. English and Norwegian.
